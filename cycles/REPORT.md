1. The ratios as stated
   ratio        value    phi^-k   k      off
   73/117      0.6239    0.6180   1    0.59pp
   44/117      0.3761    0.3820   2    0.59pp

   17/116      0.1466    0.1459   4    0.07pp
   Three of the four land within 0.6 points. 29/116 is 0.250 against 0.236 —
   the lecture says so itself: "a quarter is very close to 0.236". It is 6% off,
   and 6% of 116 days is a week.

2. Does the series actually sum?
     8 +   9 = 17   vs 17   yes
     9 +  17 = 26   vs 23   no
    17 +  23 = 40   vs 29   no
    23 +  29 = 52   vs 44   no
    29 +  44 = 73   vs 73   yes
    44 +  73 = 117  vs 117  yes
   3 of 6 consecutive triples sum. 8+9=17 holds, then it breaks for
   three in a row, then 29+44=73 and 44+73=117 hold. The two that carry the
   golden-ratio argument are the two that work; the series is not additive
   throughout, and the terms that do not fit are kept anyway.

   Seed any additive chain with two numbers, run it out, and the last four terms
